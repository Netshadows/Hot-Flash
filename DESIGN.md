# Design System: Hot Flash
**Project ID:** projects/13453545362374791117

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A "Clinical-Clean" aesthetic (Light Mode Default). The goal is clarity, trust, and professional medical grade tracking.
- **Light Mode (Default)**: Bright, airy, usage of "Snow" and "Bone White" with strong contrast charcoal text.
- **Dark Mode (Toggle/System)**: The previous "OLED Dark" empathy palette for night-time usage.


## 2. Color Palette & Roles (Flo-Inspired)
**Light Theme (Default)**
- **Background**: `#FFF9FA` (Very pale pink/white)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Primary**: `#FF5864` (Flo Pink - Warm, vibrant)
- **Primary Glare**: `#FF8090` (Soft Pink)
- **Secondary/Success**: `#FFB6C1` (Light Pink) or `#10B981` (Emerald for success data)
- **Text Main**: `#2D2D2D` (Soft Black)
- **Text Muted**: `#757575` (Warm Grey)

**Dark Theme (Overrides)**
- **Background**: `#1A1A1A`
- **Surface**: `#2D2D2D`
- **Primary**: `#FF7080` (Lighter Pink for contrast)


## 3. Typography & Accessibility
- **Family:** "Inter" (sans-serif).
- **Scale:** Base 17px.
- **Contrast:** AAA compliance required.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum 48x48px.


## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** "Soft Glass" styling. Low opacity background (rgba(142, 148, 242, 0.15)) with a 1px solid stroke. Active states glow softly.
* **Cards:** "Tactile" containers. Deep Gunmetal background with very subtle inner shadow (Neumorphic intaglio) to hold content. Rounded corners (24px).
* **Inputs:** Large, clear touch zones. Sliders use thick tracks and large thumb handles.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Mobile First:** Designed for 375px-430px width.
- **Spacious:** 24px-32px margins to let content breathe.
- **Stacked:** Vertical rhythm suitable for scrolling feeds.
