# User Personas: Hot Flash

These personas represent key user segments tailored to the **STRAW+10** staging system and the application's core symptom domains (Vasomotor, Psychological, Urogenital, Somatic).

## 1. Elena - The High-Functioning Professional
**"I need to know when my brain will work again."**

*   **Age:** 47
*   **Stage:** Late Reproductive / Early Menopause Transition (STRAW -2)
*   **Occupation:** Senior Project Manager
*   **Tech Savviness:** High (Uses productivity apps daily)

### Key Characteristics
*   **Primary Symptoms (Psychological/Vasomotor):** experiencing unpredictable "brain fog," sudden anxiety spikes before meetings, and occasional night sweats disrupting sleep.
*   **Goals:**
    *   Identify triggers for brain fog to schedule important meetings around them.
    *   Quantify sleep disruption to justify seeing a specialist.
    *   Find quick, non-hormonal relief strategies for anxiety.
*   **Pain Points:**
    *   Fear of losing professional edge.
    *   Frustrated by vague medical advice ("it's just stress").
    *   Needs data to feel in control.

### App Usage Scenario
Elena logs symptoms primarily to **correlate** them with her calendar. She uses the "Notes" feature to tag specific work events and looks for patterns in the "Psychological" domain of the Dashboard. She values the **Dark Mode** because she often logs wakefulness at 3 AM.

## 2. Maria - The Medical Monitor
**"I need accurate data to adjust my dosage."**

*   **Age:** 52
*   **Stage:** Late Menopause Transition / Early Post-menopause (STRAW -1 to +1a)
*   **Occupation:** Teacher
*   **Tech Savviness:** Moderate (Uses Facebook, WhatsApp, FitBit)

### Key Characteristics
*   **Primary Symptoms (Vasomotor/Somatic):** Intense, frequent hot flashes (10-15/day), joint pain, and fatigue. Currently trialing HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy).
*   **Goals:**
    *   Track the **frequency and severity** of hot flashes to see if the new HRT patch is working.
    *   Monitor blood pressure and weight (Somatic).
    *   Export a clear report for her 3-month follow-up with her gynecologist.
*   **Pain Points:**
    *   Memory is poor; forgets if she had a flash or just felt warm.
    *   Overwhelmed by complex interfaces; needs large buttons and high contrast.
    *   Anxious about health risks.

### App Usage Scenario
Maria uses the **Quick Log** widget on her home screen for immediate hot flash entry. She relies heavily on the **Clinician Report Export** (future feature) layout to show her doctor "Look, my severe flashes went from 10 to 4 a day." She appreciates the **Symptom Severity Scales** to distinguish between a "mild warm flush" and a "drenching sweat."

## 3. Sarah - The Long-Term Optimizer
**"I want to age strongly and comfortably."**

*   **Age:** 58
*   **Stage:** Late Post-menopause (STRAW +2)
*   **Occupation:** Retired / Volunteer
*   **Tech Savviness:** Low-Moderate (Uses iPad for reading/email)

### Key Characteristics
*   **Primary Symptoms (Urogenital/Somatic):** Vaginal dryness (GSM), Libido changes, concern for osteoporosis/heart health.
*   **Goals:**
    *   Track localized treatments and their effectiveness.
    *   Monitor long-term lifestyle habits (calcium intake, exercise).
    *   Access educational content about "healthy aging" rather than just symptom suppression.
*   **Pain Points:**
    *   Embarrassment discussing urogenital symptoms.
    *   Feels many apps only cater to the "hot flash" phase and ignore the "dryness" phase.
    *   Wants privacy and discretion.

### App Usage Scenario
Sarah uses the app less frequently (weekly) but dives deep into the **Education/Resources** hub. She tracks **Urogenital** symptoms privately to prepare for her annual checkup. She values the **Privacy-First** approach and discreet app icon.
